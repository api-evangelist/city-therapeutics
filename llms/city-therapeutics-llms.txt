# City Therapeutics

> City Therapeutics, Inc. is a privately held, clinical-stage biotechnology company in Kendall Square, Cambridge, Massachusetts, founded in 2024 by pioneers of RNA interference. It engineers next-generation RNAi trigger molecules — including proprietary cleavage-inducing tiny ("city") RNAs — paired with precision targeting ligands that deliver siRNAs beyond the liver into ocular, CNS and other extrahepatic tissues.
